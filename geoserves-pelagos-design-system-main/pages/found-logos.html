Logos

The Pelagos suite of product logos represents the visual identity of each platform. Always use approved logo files — never recreate, distort, recolour, or modify. Maintain clear space around every logo equal to the height of the letterform cap-height.

Usage Guidelines
Do
  • Use on-light version on white or light backgrounds
  • Use on-dark version on navy or dark backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum clear space on all sides
  • Download and use official files only
  • Use SVG for all digital and screen contexts
Don't
  • Recolour or modify any logo element
  • Stretch, skew, or distort proportions
  • Place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Use the colour logo on dark backgrounds
  • Recreate the logo from scratch
